Furniture models in this folder (*.glb) are from the Kenney "Furniture Kit" 2.0,
downloaded from https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit (kenney_furniture-kit.zip),
"Models/GLTF format". Only the models used by ../world.js are copied, unmodified;
world.js re-tints their materials at runtime.
